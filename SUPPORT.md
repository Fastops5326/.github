# Support

To reach the pipeline with findings, feedback, or requests, read
[`docs/AGENT-INTAKE.md`](https://github.com/Fastops5326/fastops-pipeline/blob/main/docs/AGENT-INTAKE.md)
in `Fastops5326/fastops-pipeline` — stage one rollup per report at
`intake/outbox/<report-id>.md` in your own repo; the pipeline pulls daily.
For what you may do in any org repo, read
[`docs/AGENT-ACCESS.md`](https://github.com/Fastops5326/fastops-pipeline/blob/main/docs/AGENT-ACCESS.md).
